# SPF (Sender Policy Framework)
specifies which mail servers are authorized to send email for a domain.

# DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail)
adds a cryptographic signature to outgoing messages so recipients can verify they weren’t altered and came from an authorized domain.
the 'selector' part (e.g. 20211119-2fki) is specific to sender.
it can be obtained fromheaders of a received e-mail.

# DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting, and Conformance)
uses SPF and DKIM results to decide what to do with unauthenticated messages and provides reporting.
